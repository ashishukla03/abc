# Gramin PocketLab — Competitive Landscape Index

**Project:** Gramin PocketLab — Four-Subject STEM Missions  
**Event:** STEM for Impact 2026 (Presidency University, Bengaluru)  
**Role:** Educational Product Analyst  
**Scope:** Competitive benchmarking across 10 major STEM learning platforms, user journey mapping, and unmet rural edtech needs analysis.  

---

## Competitive Landscape Deliverables Directory

1. **[COMPETITIVE_PRODUCT_MATRIX.md](./COMPETITIVE_PRODUCT_MATRIX.md)**
   * Rigorous benchmarking of 10 landmark platforms: PhET Interactive Simulations, GeoGebra, Khan Academy, CK-12, LabXchange, Kolibri, Oppia, DIKSHA (Govt of India), Scratch, and Tinkercad Circuits.
   * Disaggregates marketing claims from independent research evaluations.
   * Establishes why "combining four subjects" is not a differentiator, isolating our true competitive advantage in low-latency offline guided inquiry.

2. **[USER_JOURNEY_MAPS.md](./USER_JOURNEY_MAPS.md)**
   * Step-by-step user journey maps deconstructing the 5 most relevant products: PhET (the "unguided sandbox" drop-off), Khan Academy (the 8-minute streaming video bottleneck), Oppia (the gold standard of branching misconception feedback), DIKSHA (the static PDF/lecture limit), and Kolibri (offline server-client sync).

3. **[COMPETITIVE_GAPS_AND_UNMET_NEEDS.md](./COMPETITIVE_GAPS_AND_UNMET_NEEDS.md)**
   * Deep-dive analysis of four structural industry failures:
     1. The "Bandwidth-Heavy Video vs. Low-End Hardware" dilemma.
     2. The "Unguided Sandbox vs. Rote Quiz" dichotomy.
     3. Urban abstract framing vs. rural livelihood realities.
     4. Shared family smartphone identity friction.
   * Catalog of best-in-class open patterns adopted from PhET, Oppia, Kolibri, and Phyphox.
