# Gramin PocketLab — India & Karnataka Context Research Index

**Project:** Gramin PocketLab — Four-Subject STEM Missions  
**Event:** STEM for Impact 2026 (Presidency University, Bengaluru)  
**Role:** India-Context Research Lead  
**Scope:** Official data investigation (UDISE+ 2023–24, ASER 2023/24, PARAKH/NAS 2021, NCF-SE 2023), Karnataka state education realities, infrastructure boundaries, and five evidence-backed problem framings. **Zero code authored; zero unverified generalizations.**

   * Rigorous statistical analysis separating school facility availability from reliable/functional service:
     * Science Labs (UDISE+ 2024–25 Table 7.23): Nationally, an integrated science-lab facility was reported as available in **57.1%** of schools with secondary sections across all managements and **51.7%** of government schools with secondary sections (48.3% of government secondary sections lack dedicated facility space; earlier 50.2% draft claim designated [UNVERIFIED HISTORICAL ESTIMATE]).
     * Computer Labs: Functional computer lab access varies widely; earlier specific draft figure of 32.1% rural is designated [UNVERIFIED HISTORICAL ESTIMATE].
     * Internet (UDISE+ 2024–25 booklet): Internet facility is available in **58.6%** of government schools nationally (41.4% lack internet facility; earlier draft claims of 41.2% or 58.8% lack internet designated [UNVERIFIED HISTORICAL ESTIMATES]).
     * Learning Levels (ASER 2023): **56.7%** of rural adolescents (ages 14–18) cannot solve a basic 3-digit division problem; NAS 2021 shows Class 8 math/science drops to 42%.
     * Digital Reality (ASER 2023): **89.0%** of rural youth have a smartphone at home; among youth who can use a smartphone, **66.1%** engage in educational activities weekly (earlier claim of "only 57%" misquoted survey denominator and is designated [UNVERIFIED HISTORICAL TYPO]). Shared family phones accessed predominantly in evening.
