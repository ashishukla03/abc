# Gramin PocketLab — Learning Science Research Index

**Project:** Gramin PocketLab — Four-Subject STEM Missions  
**Event:** STEM for Impact 2026 (Presidency University, Bengaluru)  
**Role:** Learning-Science Research Lead  
**Mandate:** Evidence-led review prioritizing systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and randomized controlled trials in rural/underserved STEM education. **Zero code authored; zero empirical claims made for this prototype.**  

---

## Deliverables Directory

1. **[LEARNING_SCIENCE_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md](./LEARNING_SCIENCE_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md)**
   * In-depth evidence review covering:
     * Retrieval practice & spacing ($g \approx 0.50–0.70$, Dunlosky et al., 2013).
     * Worked examples & guided practice ($d \approx 0.60$, Sweller, 2006).
     * Formative & misconception-specific feedback ($d = 0.48$, Wisniewski, Zierer, & Hattie, 2020; Kluger & DeNisi, 1996).
     * Active learning in STEM ($g = 0.47$, Freeman et al., 2014, PNAS).
     * Visual representations & simulations (Smetana & Bell, 2012; PhET research).
     * Gamification reality check ($g = 0.49$ cognitive vs. competitive demotivation, Sailer & Homner, 2020).
     * Adaptive instruction in India ($0.37\sigma$ math gain, Muralidharan, Singh, & Ganimian, AER 2019).
     * Offline & mobile-assisted learning constraints (ASER 2023/24, UDISE+ 2023–24).
   * **7 Evidence-Based Design Principles** and **5 Critical Anti-Patterns to Avoid**.

2. **[LEARNING_SCIENCE_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv](./LEARNING_SCIENCE_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv)**
   * Structured, tabular source register documenting citations, DOIs, publication years, country settings, grade bands, sample designs, comparators, effect sizes ($d, g, \sigma$), methodological limitations, generalizability, and concrete product decisions supported.
